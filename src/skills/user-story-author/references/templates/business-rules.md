# Business-rule Description template

Use the [shared contract](../story-contract.md) and separate Title/AC fields.

## Objective
State the beneficiary/value and policy/check outcome.

## Background
Describe supplied As-Is and Why.

## Expected Behavior
State check timing, pass/fail results and flow preserved when the check does not
apply, only as supported. Mark missing applicable outcomes as unknown.

## Business Rules
Retain supplied BR IDs (or explicitly proposed local labels), applicability,
effective period, exceptions and precedence only when sourced. Do not invent
priority or effective dates.

## Scope
Identify included rule decisions and exclusions.

## Open Questions
List unresolved decisions and impact; omit when empty.
