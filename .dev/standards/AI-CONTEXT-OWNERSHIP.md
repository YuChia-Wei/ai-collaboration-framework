# Source AI Context Ownership

This document assigns owners in this source repository. The
[context boundary](AI-CONTEXT-BOUNDARY.md) governs source/project/product placement;
[SOURCE-DEVELOPMENT-POLICY.md](SOURCE-DEVELOPMENT-POLICY.md) governs execution.

## Current Owners

- `src/skills`, `src/knowledge` and `src/sub-agents` own editable reusable product.
  Component metadata and `src/distribution/manifest.yaml` declare package members.
- `.ai/core` and runtime entries are managed installation outputs. The installer
  owns generated state; `.ai/custom` holds this project's selection/customization.
- `.dev/standards` and `.dev/contracts` own this source project's governance and
  selected compatibility duties, not consumers' engineering semantics.
- `docs` owns human product manuals; `.dev/guides` explains this project's work.
- Decisions, evidence and records keep their project-selected owners. Installation
  does not adopt rules, execute a role, authorize a change or prove acceptance.

Reusable semantic identity and strength are defined in
`src/knowledge/engineering-common/references/ENGINEERING-IDENTITY-CONTRACT.md`
and `CONTEXT-RESOURCE-OWNERSHIP.md`, with technology selections in the same
source package. Do not make a consumer depend on this project policy.

## Compatible Legacy Registry

`AI-CONTEXT-OWNERSHIP.yaml` retains legacy identity classification and governance
term routing for interpreting historical and explicitly selected published-format
records. Its `.ai/assets` paths are absent from this source tree. It is not the
current product manifest or an executable ordinary-source resolver. A legacy
operation needs its compatible revision, policy and actual available tools.

Keep stable IDs and subject-qualified state: repository integration, workflow
completion, assessment finality, source validation, publication and downstream
adoption are separate facts. Historical wording and provenance remain history;
a project registry does not become portable authority through packaging.
