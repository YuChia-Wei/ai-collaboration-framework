program uses F: for disposable work and preserves `F:/ai-context-tests` entirely.

drive silently. Necessary real filesystem/process and durable recovery evidence
has its own selected scope; RAM alone cannot preserve the only recovery copy.

persistent repository-local ignored recovery/backup root. Do not create them or

an actual selected config-1 input. No whole-repository copies/history matrices.
